About KB Music

Discover the story behind the music and the vision that drives us forward.

Our Kenyan Story

KB Music was founded by Kraizy Bwoy in Nairobi, Kenya with a simple yet powerful vision: to create exceptional musical experiences that resonate with audiences and elevate events to new heights.

What started as a passion project has grown into a full-service music brand, offering everything from music production to live performances and event services across Kenya and beyond.

Mission & Vision

Our mission is to elevate music and create unforgettable event experiences through innovation, creativity, and technical excellence.

We envision a world where every event is transformed by the power of exceptional sound and musical artistry, showcasing the best of Kenyan talent.

Kraizy Bwoy

Founder & Creative Director
